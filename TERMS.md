Effective September 15, 2026.

The Coursekin software is provided under the [MIT License](LICENSE), including its warranty disclaimer and limitation of liability. No separate Coursekin account or subscription is required. OpenAI API usage, ChatGPT or Codex access and any associated charges follow your agreement with those providers.

Coursekin helps explain course material and practice reasoning. Generated answers can be wrong or incomplete. Check important dates, assessment rules and claims against your original syllabus, textbook and instructor. Coursekin does not represent a school or make grading or enrollment decisions.

Use materials you have permission to use and follow your class rules for AI assistance. Sharing the software does not grant permission to redistribute a textbook or another person's files. The software license covers Coursekin code and original project assets, not your uploaded materials or third party works.

The local app is intended for one person on their own computer. It is not configured as a public or multiuser service. Plugin behavior depends on the capabilities and policies of its host. Features, compatibility and availability can change.

For help or bug reports, use [Coursekin support](https://github.com/agammann/coursekin/issues). Do not post API keys, private assignments, textbooks or personal records in public issues.
