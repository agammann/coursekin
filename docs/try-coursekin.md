# Try Coursekin

[Back to Coursekin](../README.md)

Use this fictional Biology 101 class before adding your own files. This is original sample material, not a real textbook or syllabus. Answers require a configured local API key or access through your plugin host.

## Add the class

In the local app, choose **New class**, enter **Biology 101 practice**, and select **Paste text instead** for both sources. In the plugin, provide the class name and paste both sources with their labels.

### Textbook sample

```text
Biology 101 practice textbook
Section 1: Photosynthesis

Photosynthesis converts light energy into chemical energy. In plants,
chloroplasts contain thylakoid membranes and a fluid called the stroma.
The light dependent reactions take place in the thylakoid membranes.
They use light and water and produce oxygen, ATP and NADPH.
The Calvin cycle takes place in the stroma. It uses ATP and NADPH
from the light dependent reactions to help fix carbon dioxide into sugars.
```

### Syllabus sample

Select **Start asking questions** in the local app, or ask the plugin to confirm that it can read both sources.

## Ask and check

| Ask | What the supplied material supports |
| :--- | :--- |
| “What percentage is the final exam worth? Show me the source.” | 40 percent, from the syllabus |
| “Where do the light dependent reactions happen?” | The thylakoid membranes, from the textbook |
| “How do the light dependent reactions help the Calvin cycle?” | They supply ATP and NADPH, from the textbook |
| “What is the late work policy?” | The sample does not provide one; Coursekin should say so |
| “Quiz me on photosynthesis, one question at a time.” | A grounded practice question, followed by a pause for your attempt |

Open a citation or source button in the local app to inspect its excerpt. In a plugin host, check the source location it provides. Wording varies; compare the facts and sources rather than expecting an exact answer.
